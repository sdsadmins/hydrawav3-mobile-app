## Issue 1 — Apple flagged the app as a "medical app" (Guideline 1.4.1)

Because the app connects to and controls the Hydra hardware, Apple currently treats it as a **medical device app**, which requires official regulatory proof.

We have **two possible paths — please choose one.**

### Option A — Reposition as a wellness / recovery product (no documents needed)
- We remove all **"medical," "clinical," "therapy," "treatment," and "pain"** language from the app and the store listing, and present Hydra as a general **muscle-recovery / wellness / relaxation** product.
- ✅ Fastest, lowest cost, no documents required, fully in our control.
- ⚠️ Only valid if Hydra can legitimately be marketed as a **non-medical wellness** product. If the device is legally registered/sold as a **medical device**, this path is not appropriate and could create compliance issues.
- **What I need from you:** written confirmation that we can market Hydra as a general wellness/recovery device (not medical/therapeutic).

### Option B — Keep it as a medical app and submit documentation
Apple will then require **all** of the following from the device's regulatory records:
1. **Regulatory clearance** for the hardware — e.g. **FDA clearance / 510(k)** (USA), **CE mark certificate** (Europe), or equivalent.
2. A **hardware test report or peer-reviewed study (PDF)** proving the device works as described.
   *(Note: a user manual does NOT qualify — Apple specifically excludes that.)*
3. The **list of countries** where Hydra has regulatory clearance (so we either limit the app to those countries or state it in the description).
- ✅ Keeps medical positioning and claims.
- ⚠️ Requires the official documents above, which only you / the manufacturer can provide.
- **What I need from you:** the documents in points 1 and 2, plus the country list in point 3.

## Issue 2 — Apple requires a demo video (Guideline 2.1)

Because the app only works with a physical Hydra device, Apple cannot test it on their own and **requires a short demo video**. Since the live hardware is on your side, **please record this video using one of your Hydra devices and an iPhone or iPad** — it's quickest done where the working device is.

> Apple needs a **new video for every future update** too, so it's good to set this up on your end.

**Please follow the script below exactly** — it's built to cover everything Apple asked for.
**Target length: 2–4 minutes.**

### What you'll need
- 1 iPhone or iPad with the app installed via **TestFlight** (I'll send the build).
- 1 working Hydra device, powered on and ready to pair.
- A second phone (optional but recommended) to film the Hydra device physically responding.
- A quiet, well-lit space.

### How to screen-record on iPhone / iPad
1. **Settings → Control Center →** add **Screen Recording**.
2. Swipe down from the top-right to open Control Center → tap the **record (●)** button → wait 3 seconds.
3. When finished, swipe down again and tap the red bar to **stop**. The video saves to **Photos**.

> **Tip:** Keep the Hydra device **in camera view** (or film it on a second phone) so Apple can see the hardware actually turning on / running when the app sends commands.

### The script — record these steps in order

| # | Step | What to show |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | **Launch the app** on the iPhone/iPad (show the home screen first, so it's clearly a real device). | App opening on a physical device. |
| 2 | **Allow permission prompts** as they appear — tap **Allow** on the **Bluetooth** prompt (and Notifications if it appears). | Each permission pop-up clearly visible. |
| 3 | **Log in** with the test account. | Login screen → home. |
| 4 | Go to the **Devices** tab → **Bluetooth**. Tap to **scan / pair** with the Hydra device. Wait until it connects. | The device appearing in the list and connecting. |
| 5 | Hold up / film the **physical Hydra device** so it is visible. | The real hardware on camera. |
| 6 | Select a **protocol** and **start a session** on the connected device. | Protocol selection → session starting. |
| 7 | Show the **Hydra device actually responding** (turning on / running) when the session starts. | Hardware reacting to the app — **this is the key shot Apple wants.** |

| 9 | Open the **History** tab to show the completed session was recorded. | Session history. |
| 10 | *(Optional)* Briefly show **Settings**. | App is complete / functional. |

### Recording do's and don'ts

- ✅ Show **every permission prompt** and the **hardware physically responding**.
- ✅ Keep it steady; no need to talk — on-screen actions are enough.
- ❌ Don't speed up or cut out the **pairing** step — Apple specifically wants to see it.
